## Purpose

Express a `CONTENT` as an autonomous professional text while preserving thesis, evidence, utility, and limits.

## Use when

The content supports a useful explanation, decision, distinction, experience, or procedure without requiring a visual demonstration.

## Do not use when

The promise depends on seeing an execution, interface, temporal comparison, or result that text cannot represent faithfully. Do not fabricate experience, metrics, clients, or outcomes.

## Supported shapes

- `post`: one main idea with the context required to understand it;
- `long_post`: a longer development only when evidence and explanation justify it.

## Voice and packaging

Use the configured workspace voice. Do not add viral cadence, engagement questions, dramatization, or performative authority by default. The opening identifies a real problem or consequence; the body delivers the promised reasoning; the ending completes the utility without relying on a missing resource.

## Review

Review fidelity, clarity, evidence, preserved limits, and user-supplied response. Observations remain associated with the OUTPUT until an approved LEVEL-UP changes the product profile.
