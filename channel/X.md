## Purpose

Express a `CONTENT` as an autonomous and precise text without discarding evidence or limits to force brevity.

## Use when

One main idea can stand as a short publication, or the evidence supports a complete long-form development.

## Do not use when

Compression removes essential conditions, changes meaning, or turns a visually dependent explanation into an unsupported assertion. Do not create a thread by routine.

## Supported shapes

- `short_post`: one complete idea with indispensable limits;
- `long_post`: a longer post only when the CONTENT justifies it.

## Voice and packaging

Use the configured workspace voice. Do not imitate viral cadence, exaggerate certainty, add confrontation, or request interaction by default. Open with the real idea, problem, or consequence; develop what is needed to deliver the promise; close without relying on a missing link.

## Review

Review fidelity, context loss, evidence, limits, and user-supplied response. Observations remain associated with the OUTPUT until sufficient evidence and an approved LEVEL-UP change the profile.
