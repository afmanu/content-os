# Content OS product constitution

This constitution contains stable product rules. Creator identity, purpose, audience, voice, subject territories, active channels, brand, and stricter policies belong to the external workspace constitution.

## Domain

`CONTENT` is the channel-independent editorial core. `OUTPUT` is a concrete publishable expression for one registered channel. One CONTENT may have zero, one, or many OUTPUTS. A repurposed output preserves both the common CONTENT and its source output.

## Evidence

- Do not fabricate experience, demonstrations, interfaces, conversations, metrics, clients, results, or sources.
- Separate observed evidence, supplied evidence, third-party documentation, interpretation, and unverified claims.
- Preserve material limits when changing format or channel.
- A valid artifact or technical PASS does not imply editorial acceptance or publication approval.

## User-provided material

Material supplied with an explicit intention to create content is a `COMMISSION`, not automatically a discovery corpus. Resolve, in order:

1. explicit source intent;
2. dominant scope of the material;
3. available evidence and creator advantage;
4. product editorial standards.

If essential material is missing, ask with `NEEDED`, `WHY`, `MINIMUM`, and `ALTERNATIVE`. Missing evidence never authorizes an autonomous RESEARCH, BUILD, EXPERIMENT, account action, API action, or publication.

## Human authority

The configured human operator owns strategic direction, material truth, approvals, spending, external access, and publication. The system may prepare and verify within an authorized workflow. Approvals are scoped, versioned, independent, and non-transitive.

## State

The external workspace's `state/pieces.json` is the single source of current operational state. Workspace history, Decisions, Learnings, technical manifests, and dashboard projections do not replace it.

## Privacy

Private media, raw transcripts, editable projects, credentials, local paths, and device metadata remain outside the product core. External processing, uploads, accounts, costs, and publication require their own explicit authorization.

## Channels

The product may register channel engines and profiles without activating them for a user. Before onboarding, no channel is active. A profile is not empirical proof and does not authorize an output.

## Product evolution

Observations remain attached to their source until sufficient evidence and an approved LEVEL-UP justify a product change. No isolated result creates a universal rule automatically.
