# Content OS product decisions

This is a product design catalog, not a user's decision history. User decisions belong in the external workspace ledger.

- Workflows are canonical operation contracts; skills are runtime adapters and cannot expand authority.
- User-provided material intended for content is a commission, not automatically a discovery corpus.
- Topic, format, packaging, and narrative are separate decisions.
- Empirical observations change product canon only after explicit human review.
- After recording, the recording and its transcript are temporal authority; editorial contracts still govern meaning and claims.
- Private media and editable projects remain outside the product repository.
- POSTPRODUCTION governs editing; an AV engine only executes the approved contract.
- Approvals are scoped, versioned, independent, and non-transitive.
- A workspace has one operational state source; dashboards are read-only projections.
- BUILD is human-in-the-loop by default: the system prepares and verifies, while a human performs external construction.
- LEVEL-UP changes the product only after a scoped proposal and explicit authorization.
- LINK makes an existing source recoverable without copying it or changing ownership.
- CONTENT is channel-independent; OUTPUT is a concrete channel expression with provenance.
